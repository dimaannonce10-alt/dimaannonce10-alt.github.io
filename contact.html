How do I restore my VIP Pass after switching devices?

Open BetX Royal, navigate to the VIP Pass screen, and tap "Restore Purchases". StoreKit 2 will query Apple's App Store servers and immediately re-activate your active VIP status.

How do I cancel an auto-renewing VIP subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed directly through Apple Inc. To cancel, go to your iPhone's Settings > [Your Apple ID Name] > Subscriptions > BetX Royal > Cancel Subscription at least 24 hours prior to the next billing date.

How do I request complete deletion of my data?

BetX Royal operates with a privacy-first, client-side architecture; your game progression is stored locally on your device and not on remote user account servers. You can permanently erase all local game data at any time simply by uninstalling the application from your iPhone.
